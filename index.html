Yu Zhao

PhD Student · School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh and a member of EdinburghNLP, supervised by Pasquale Minervini, Mirella Lapata, and Fazl Barez.

My research focuses on large language models: how pre-training data shapes them, how they use knowledge and long contexts, and how to make them more efficient and controllable. I am currently working on efficient and faithful vision-language model agents, and on building interpretability agents that automatically discover the mechanisms of LLMs.
